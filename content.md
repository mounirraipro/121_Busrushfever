# Bus Rush Fever

Bus Rush Fever is a browser bus parking challenge focused on smooth movement, tight spaces, and steady decisions. The page describes the browser version plainly and avoids official-app claims, guaranteed rewards, and pressure to install software.

## Copy Direction

Keep the page useful, human, and suitable for a general audience. Focus on how the browser game works, how to play safely, and how players can improve through practice.

## Core Play Advice

Make wide turns, correct early, and avoid rushing when the parking lane gets narrow.
